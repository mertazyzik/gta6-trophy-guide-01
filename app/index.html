    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough

    Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your platinum save. There is no mission replay, so keep saves at the start and end of each chapter — the game offers 15 manual save slots. Progress toward the 100% checklist counts even before the checklist becomes visible.

    Missable trophies

    Only Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is potentially missable, and that is unconfirmed: the first meditation, on Jason's house balcony with Lucia after the mission “Down to Earth” in Chapter 1, may be required. Do it in Chapter 1 to be safe. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free-roam.
